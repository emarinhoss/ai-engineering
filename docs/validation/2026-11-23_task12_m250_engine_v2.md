## Method

Each member solves pi_c and Tt4 so that take-off shaft SFC and the inter-turbine temperature Tt4.5 = Tt4 tau_m1 tau_tH both equal the brochure (MGT is measured between the gas-producer and power turbines). Part power uses shaft_power_at with pi_c not above its sea-level rated value (a ceiling: pi_c falls with Tt4 at part power). Brochure values are maxima, and the 450 column is read as the B17F (see label_conflict in engines.yaml).

## What the validation shows and does not show (review M-3)

Once take-off SFC is calibrated, the part-power SFC is nearly fixed by the cycle, so the SFC comparison is weak evidence. The claim made is only that the model is **consistent with the brochure from 70 to 100 % power at sea level**. It is not shown at lower power (the brochure has no point below 70 %) or at any altitude. The 380 and 377 shp points are effectively one point. The ratios to take-off SFC and the MGT drop discriminate more (tables below). Wrong calibrations pass the SFC comparison as well.

## Table cells outside the choked-turbine assumption (review M-1)

App. K holds the HP turbine ratios fixed because stations 4 and 4.5 are choked. In this engine's table **42.8 % of the 2717 cells** (altitude x speed x power fraction) have an LP turbine pressure ratio below the critical value. The cells are choked only at high power: over the (altitude, speed) columns the lowest power fraction at which the cell is choked is 0.45 to 0.55 of the table's full-throttle power (fraction axis 0.10 to 1.00); every cell below the smaller of the two fractions is unchoked, at every altitude and speed. The validation covers 70 to 100 % power at sea level only. The share of a mission's fuel burned in unchoked cells is reported by Stage 1 (`unchoked_fuel_share` of the design record).

## The brochure's column labels (review M-6)

The brochure prints 450 shp under 'B17C' and 420 shp under 'B17F, F/C, F/2'; FAA TCDS E10CE gives the B17F series 450 shp. This work reads the labels as swapped and takes the 450 column as the B17F. **That is not confirmed by Rolls-Royce.** If the 420 column were the B17F, its take-off SFC is +7.2 % worse than the 450 column's, and the validation fails (0 of 9 members pass, see the 420 column above).
