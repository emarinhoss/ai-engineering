- Level flight at V_C needs 23.3 % of the sea-level full-throttle power at the lightest weight; without the limit the sea-level level top speed is 185.2 kt EAS against V_D 165.0 kt EAS (above V_D). The power limit carries the protection at low altitude.
- EP-3's margin is set by the negative gust at payload off with ballast and shrinks with altitude (S-3); above 23,000 ft it is smaller still, and from 27,500 ft V_C itself is not cleared with the held gust (payload off with ballast).

- EP-7: the structure is cleared above V_C only for the ruled rule's falling gust; a level-flight gust of 30 ft/s at V_D exceeds the limits (P5 row 4), which is why EP-3's excursion is set with the held gust.

## 7. Assumptions

- Weights are P5's: r0's take-off and zero-fuel weight, and payload off with P4b's ballast at the payload station at zero and at full fuel. Payload off without ballast is not flown (P4b) and is not a case. The empty-weight band is not applied.
- EP-2: level flight at V_C with r0's polar (CD0 built up at the speed, K of the record) and r0's App. L propeller, as Stage 1's level-speed code; the full-throttle power is P0's table through r0's engine model at the speed and altitude. The limit that holds at every flown weight is the smallest per-weight value. Climb and descent are not covered by a power limit; the speed hold (EP-1) covers them.
- EP-2's second reading uses P6's representative blade at constant speed, iterated until its thrust equals the drag. P6 failed its validation row 5, so the reading has no weight and is not used in r1.
- EP-3: the allowed excursion is found with the gust held at its V_C value at the excursion speed (PD-24 item 1); the spec's reading, the ruled rule's gust at the speed, is computed beside it. The limits are P5 row 3's. The stated value is the smallest over the flown weights at and below EP-8's altitude limit, rounded down to one decimal.
- EP-4: point mass on a straight path at a constant descent angle, entered at V_C without a pushover; lift W cos(gamma); idle is zero thrust (no windmilling drag credited); full power is P0's table with the App. L propeller. Each case stops at V_D, at sea level or at the choices file's time limit.
- EP-5: the command limits are R9's limits less the ledger's gust margin (margin_gust_g) on each side (PD-24 item 2). R16's turn is available if n = 1/cos(bank) is inside the positive command limit and below the stall-limited load factor at r0's loiter speed, the mission altitude and the loiter start weight.
- EP-6: V_stall in EAS with the sea-level ISA density, as Stage 1's own clean stall speed takes it; C_L,max does not change with Mach or Reynolds number here. P2's measured clean maximum lift is the r1 value; r0's is the record's.
- EP-8: the protection's altitude limit is R12's ceiling requirement (PD-24 item 3). The engine's maximum operating altitude and relight envelope are declared assumptions under R-f, with no value from any source.
- r1 findings: every weight raised by P4b's r1 empty-weight change; r0's polar and wing are kept, so r1's longer aft body (PD-21) is not in the drag.
- A trial of the code was run on 2026-11-28 before the record, after the choices file was written and before any commit of it. It changed two things: the reason given in the choices file for the EP-3 reading said the gust load factor does not rise above V_C under the ruled rule, the trial showed that it rises slightly (the lift slope grows with Mach number), and the reason was rewritten; and the code's stall speed was changed from the constant sea-level density of conceptual/units.py to the sea-level ISA density, which Stage 1 uses (they differ in the fifth digit).
