## 1. What was asked

Four questions (spec section 1): the endurance-against-mass Pareto front for a 0.5 kg payload and
its drivers; whether active learning beats Latin hypercube and random sampling in six dimensions,
measured on a pool replay and against the exact front of the low-fidelity model; whether a
two-level model reaches a held-out error with fewer SU2 polars than SU2 alone; and the longest
endurance design under the 4.0 kg class C2 cap with P(mass <= cap) >= 0.9, confirmed by one SU2
polar. Every answer is reported whichever way it goes.

## 4. Question 2: active learning against space-filling sampling

Two measurements, both on the low-fidelity model, both published.

**Pool replay** (`runs/replay_drone_pool/2026-11-08/replay.csv`, 192-point low pool, 10 seeds,
8 initial points): EHVI recovers the whole 15-point pool front by 32 evaluations (regret 0.000)
where random and LHS are at 0.118 and 0.107 after 40 and max-variance at 0.062. The easy case: a
smooth model, no live gates.

**Online, unrestricted candidates, gates live** (`runs/replay_drone_online/2026-11-08/online.csv`
and `2026-11-08_feas/online.csv`, regret against the exact front of the 9^6 grid,
`runs/drone_grid/2026-11-08/front.csv`, 249 points from 531,441 evaluations, 459,432 clean):

| n = 40 | random | lhs | al_ehvi | al_ehvi_feas | al_maxvar | al_maxvar_feas |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| regret | 0.294 | 0.271 | 0.317 | **0.092** | 0.353 | 0.268 |
| gated picks of 32 | 1.9 | 4.0 | 29.7 | 7.5 | 25.4 | 7.6 |

Plain EHVI **loses** to random and LHS because 30 of its 32 picks fail a gate: the front lies
against the gated edge of the box and the GPs know nothing about the gates. Weighting the
acquisition by a logistic feasibility model fitted on the evaluated points takes EHVI to a third of
LHS's regret; the same weighting lifts max-variance only to parity. The answer on this problem:
active learning beats space-filling sampling only when the acquisition knows where the design is
buildable.

## 6. Question 3: two-level against single-level — ANSWERED ON ALL 32 DESIGNS

`analysis/mf_drone.py` fits the two-level model (every low-pool point as the low level, n SU2
designs as the high level) against a GP on the SU2 designs alone, held-out error against n. All 32
pool designs are complete after decision 9 (Éder, 2026-11-10), which accepted the budget-exhausted
runs of designs 8 and 28 only; their sections carry the flag `budget_exhausted_accepted=3`, and
design 8's accepted runs drift up to 54.1 % in cl and 44.8 % in cd. Budgets 8, 12, 16 and 24 form
(each must leave four held out); the record is `runs/drone_mf/2026-11-09/mf.csv`, ten seeds. Mean
over seeds:

| n SU2 designs | held out | endurance RMSE: raw low | SU2 only | two-level | two-level wins | 90 % coverage: SU2 only | two-level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 24 | 2.299 h | 1.525 h | **0.824 h** | 8 of 10 | 0.43 | 0.61 |
| 12 | 20 | 2.299 h | 1.112 h | **0.808 h** | 6 of 10 | 0.62 | 0.60 |
| 16 | 16 | 2.299 h | 0.865 h | **0.747 h** | 5 of 10 | 0.71 | 0.68 |
| 24 | 8 | 2.299 h | **0.704 h** | 0.726 h | 5 of 10 | 0.78 | 0.69 |

**The answer.** At small budgets, yes: with 8 SU2 designs the two-level endurance error is 0.824 h
against 1.525 h for SU2 alone, and SU2 alone first reaches it at 24 designs. The advantage shrinks
as the SU2 budget grows: the seeds split 5 to 5 at 16, and at 24 SU2 alone is slightly better on
the mean. On the interval, no: neither model's 90 % interval covers 90 % of the held-out designs at
any budget (0.43 to 0.78). On MTOW the two-level relative RMSE is 0.0021 to 0.0024 against 0.0071 to
0.0869 for SU2 alone, with the raw low model at 0.0030: mass is a low-fidelity quantity. The fitted
endurance slope ρ averages 0.87 to 0.89 per budget. On 30 designs (before decision 9) the mean
advantage held at every budget (SU2 alone reached the 8-design two-level error of 0.732 h at 24
designs, 0.702 h); adding designs 8 and 28 (SU2 endurance 37.9 % and 48.2 % below the low model)
is the only difference between the two readings. The 30-design table is kept in the spec's dated blocks.

## 8. Biases that travel with every number

From the spec (section 13) and the record: no trim drag; fully turbulent skin friction on the
non-wing parts above Re 5e5; a fixed propulsive efficiency; the closed-form structure; one section
Reynolds number per design while the chord varies along the span; the low-fidelity section's
`section_lift_effectiveness` is `assumed` at 0.75 with band [0.6, 0.9] while the E387 tunnel
implies 0.93 to 0.97 (V6f; a ledger ruling, open); the high level's own V6e miss (decision 8); and
the Cauchy-stopped runs' drift (1 to 2 % in cl, 4 to 7 % in cd at Re 203,800). The one-at-a-time
sweep of the fixed quantities' bands at the inverse answer (`analysis/bias_sweep_drone.py`) is
PENDING on that answer; at the low-fidelity answer (`runs/drone_bias_lf/2026-11-09/`) the three
largest are skin areal density, propulsive efficiency and pack specific energy, each worth 13 to
17 % of endurance across its band.

**Coarse against medium (recorded 2026-11-09, `runs/pool_af/2026-11-09/bias.csv`):** ten pairs on
designs 0 to 3; medium over coarse cl 0.995 to 1.028, cd 0.945 to 1.016. The coarse pool's drag is a
few percent high in the working range; lift is mesh-converged to the drift band.
