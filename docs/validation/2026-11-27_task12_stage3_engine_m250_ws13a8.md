## 1. What these rows are

The record was sized with the table of the M250 family's nominal member (spec 7.1b: the table is the App. K cycle itself). So the power, SFC and ceiling rows compare the table with a family that contains it. They are **reported, not judged**. The independent engine check is the brochure's part-power SFC, which the take-off calibration did not use (7.1b item 3); that row is judged.

## 6. Caveats carried

- The M250 table is validated at sea level, static, and 70-100 % power only (brochure rows). Mission cruise and loiter run at 13-27 % of the rating, at 20,000 ft.
- 77.4 % of the baseline's mission fuel burns in unchoked cells, outside App. K's off-design assumption.
- The 23,000 ft ceiling requirement is above the certified 20,000 ft B17 installations (docs/validation/2026-11-24_task12_m250_r6_sources.md); the maximum operating altitude and relight envelope are design assumptions (owner ruling R-f).
- Installation losses (inlet, exhaust, bleed, accessory power) are not modelled beyond the 1.02 SFC allowance.
- Not done: re-sizing the design with the family's SFC (the table is the nominal member; the +-10 % SFC and power bands are carried by the info-gap study).
