## 4. Consequence

Stage 1 froze W0 = 2,806 lb, fuel 756 lb, using SFC 0.901 on every leg, and a service ceiling of 26,085 ft.

Re-sized with the App. K leg-average SFC (cruise 1.081, loiter 1.084) and nothing else changed: W0 = 3,199 lb, fuel 986 lb. The TP100's 241 hp then **does not meet** every power requirement (largest: ceiling, 253 hp sea-level equivalent, with Stage 1's lapse).

With the App. K power the frozen design's service ceiling is 24,884-24,901 ft against R12's 25,000 ft.

Under spec 7.1a a row outside tolerance that takes a requirement margin below zero returns the design to Stage 1. The ceiling row does that; the SFC rows would once the extra fuel is carried.
