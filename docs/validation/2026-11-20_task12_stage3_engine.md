## 4. Consequence

Stage 1 froze W0 = 2,806 lb, fuel 756 lb, using SFC 0.901 on every leg, and a service ceiling of 26,085 ft.

Re-sized with the App. K leg-average SFC (cruise 1.159, loiter 1.162) and nothing else changed: W0 = 3,391 lb, fuel 1,099 lb. The TP100's 241 hp then **does not meet** every power requirement (largest: ceiling, 266 hp sea-level equivalent, with Stage 1's lapse).

With the App. K power the frozen design's service ceiling is 24,898-24,917 ft against R12's 25,000 ft.

Under spec 7.1a a row outside tolerance that takes a requirement margin below zero returns the design to Stage 1. The ceiling row does that; the SFC rows would once the extra fuel is carried.

Sensitivity: calibrated instead to the unverified 0.847 take-off extract, the loiter SFC runs 1.042 at the start and 1.128 at the end of the leg (family means); the shortfall against 0.901 remains.

Brochure facts that bear on the design but are not measured here: the starting envelope ends at 19,685 ft, below the 20,000 ft loiter, so an in-flight relight needs a descent; the output shaft turns at 2,158 rpm against the ledger's assumed 2,000 rpm (inside its band).

## 5. Not done in this run

- No TP100 part-power datum to validate the part-throttle trend: the owner's brochure gives none. The maker would have to be asked for a part-power curve.
- Installation losses (inlet, exhaust, bleed, accessory power) are not modelled; they would lower power and raise SFC further.
- Power at altitude uses 95 kt for every row; the ceiling bisection uses the same speed.
