## 10. Decisions and assumptions

- PD-25 (owner ruling): P9 on Opus 5.5 before Fable's review. PD-26: how the findings enter r1. PD-27: the sponson.
- The integrated climb is P0's integration (prelim/p0_baseline.integrate_climb) coded in conceptual/uav.py; a test shows they agree to 1e-12 on r0. A member of U(h) that has no rate of climb at some altitude cannot fly the mission (info_gap.UNFLYABLE).
- Stage 1 without the r1 entries reproduces every r0 number (the full test suite passes on the new code).
- The info-gap study uses 4 processes (Gate B used 2): each job carries its own seed, so the result does not depend on it.
- 'The lightest design' is Stage 1 at the bisection's lower end (8 h, the nominal R5 requirement) with r1's ledger. It is not chosen and is not r1; it is recorded so that the failure has numbers.
- The diagnosis's climb check is the integrated climb's own test (rate of climb at each altitude above zero) evaluated at the weight after take-off; a test shows it agrees with the mission on whether a member can be flown.
