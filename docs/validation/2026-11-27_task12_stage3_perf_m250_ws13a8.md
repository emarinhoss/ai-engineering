## Rows outside tolerance, not tuned

- **installed power, take-off over 50 ft in the field length**, -11.62 % against +-5 %. - **take-off weight, Anderson first estimate (empty fraction 0.62)**, +114.88 % against +-10 %. Owner ruling G-2: counted as explained, not failed.
