# Task 4 replay on the committed pool, 2026-11-02 (interim: 67 of 92 pool points)

Written by Fable 5.1 from `runs/replay/2026-11-02/replay.csv` (produced by
`python -m surrogate.replay --results runs/pool/results.csv --out runs/replay/2026-11-02/replay.csv --n-max 48`,
log in the same folder) and from `docs/validation/2026-11-02_replay_summary.md` (medians over seeds, from
`python -m postprocess.plot_replay`). Every number below is in one of those two files. Nothing was tuned
after seeing the result.

## Setup

- Pool: the 67 pool runs committed in `runs/pool/` at 08:00 UTC, all with status converged (the 25
  remaining points of the 92-point design were still running on Colab). The replay is to be rerun on the
  complete pool; the verdicts below are interim.
- Ground truth: `recovery_total` and `q_peak_W_m2` from each record. Objectives in minimisation form are
  `-recovery_total` and `log q_peak`. The true pool front has 22 non-dominated points of 67.
- Strategies: `random`, `lhs` (fresh maximin Latin hypercube per budget, nearest unused pool point),
  `al_ehvi` (Monte-Carlo expected hypervolume improvement), `al_maxvar` (largest predictive variance). All
  start from the same 8 random points for a given seed, 10 seeds (0 to 9), one point per step to n = 48.
- Metrics on the held-out pool points at n in {8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48}: RMSE of recovery (absolute),
  relative RMSE of q_peak, normalised hypervolume regret, interval coverage at 50/80/90/95 percent.
  At n = 48 the held-out set has 19 points, so one point is 5.3 percent of coverage.
- GP: Matern 5/2 with ARD, one GP per objective, fitted by marginal likelihood at every step of the
  active strategies. The fitted noise variance is 0 at every budget for both objectives (only the 1e-6
  floor remains), consistent with CFD outputs that are deterministic to far below the model error.

Figures: `docs/validation/2026-11-02_replay_error_vs_n.png` (median and interquartile band over seeds)
and `docs/validation/2026-11-02_replay_calibration.png` (coverage at n = 48).

## Findings

1. Hypervolume regret (the quantity EHVI is built to reduce): `al_ehvi` is best at every budget. Its
   median regret is 0.072 at n = 12 against 0.140 (random), 0.106 (lhs) and 0.115 (al_maxvar), and it is
   exactly 0 in 10 of 10 seeds from n = 32 on, meaning the 32 selected points already contain the whole
   22-point true front. At n = 48 the others are still at 0.024 (random), 0.012 (lhs) and 0.0024
   (al_maxvar).
2. Held-out error (global accuracy): `al_ehvi` does not win here until the budget is large. Up to n = 24
   it is the worst or second worst strategy on RMSE of recovery (1.56e-4 at n = 24 against 6.1e-5 for lhs
   and 3.7e-5 for al_maxvar) and on relative RMSE of q_peak (8.9e-4 against 5.1e-4 and 4.0e-4). It
   overtakes random and lhs at n = 32 and is level with al_maxvar at n = 40 and 48 (recovery RMSE
   1.3e-5 to 1.4e-5, q_peak relative RMSE 3.5e-4). This is the expected trade-off: EHVI spends its runs
   near the front and learns the rest of the box last. `al_maxvar` is the best global learner at every
   budget, and plain `lhs` beats `random` throughout.
3. Scale of the errors: at n = 48 every strategy predicts recovery to about 1e-5 to 5e-5 absolute (the
   pool spans 0.256 to 0.406) and q_peak to 0.04 percent relative. The response surfaces are very smooth
   over this box, so the surrogate problem is easy and the practical gain of any strategy over random
   is a factor of a few in error at fixed n, not an order of magnitude.
4. Calibration at n = 48: the recovery intervals are too wide. Median empirical coverage is 0.84 to
   0.95 at the 50 percent level and 0.95 to 1.0 at the 80 to 95 percent levels for all strategies, so
   the 90 percent interval covers 95 to 100 percent of held-out points, above the 97 percent edge of
   the C1 flag band. For q_peak the intervals are close to nominal: 0.66 to 0.79 at the 50 percent level,
   0.84 to 0.92 at 80 percent, 0.89 to 0.95 at 90 and 95 percent, inside the 80 to 97 percent band.
   The over-coverage on recovery is the conservative direction (errors smaller than the model says) and
   is reported as a finding; with 19 held-out points and a zero fitted noise, it is consistent with a
   lengthscale that is slightly short for a surface this smooth. No hyperparameter was changed.
5. Cost: one EHVI step (two GP fits plus the acquisition over the unselected pool) takes 1.4 s on the
   container CPU at n = 48; random and lhs take no fitting time between report budgets.

## Verdict against the PLAN gate

Active learning with EHVI beats random and LHS on the metric it targets (hypervolume regret) at every
budget and in every seed from n = 32. It does not beat them on global held-out error below n = 32, and
the max-variance rule is the better choice when the goal is a globally accurate surrogate. Both
statements go on the slide. The interval calibration is good for q_peak and conservative for recovery.
