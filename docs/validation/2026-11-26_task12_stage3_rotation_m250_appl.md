## Method and assumptions

- Wing height h = h_cg - 0.1 D_f (the render draws the wing at -0.1 D_f); this is an assumption, so h +- 1 ft is shown.
- Ground-roll attitude: the angle where the free-air lattice CL equals the ledger cl_ground; k_GE = CL(ground)/CL(free) at that angle (1.25 deg nominal). Planar lattice (fine), ground plane parallel to the wing plane, by the method of images.
- k_GE multiplies the ledger's cl_ground and flap_delta_cl_max, so both wing lift terms of the rotation balance (ground-attitude lift and the take-off flap increment) scale. Scaling the flap increment by the wing's factor is an approximation; the flap's own ground effect was not computed.
- Everything else in uav.layout (weight, thrust, main-wheel reaction and friction, CG, wing moment, tail geometry) is Stage 1's. The main-wheel load changes through the lift terms.
- Layout check: with k_GE = 1 the rebuilt layout gives -0.982328022 against the record's -0.982328021 (relative difference 1.5e-09).
- Validation of the ground-effect model: see the section below. The lift gain, which is what this row uses, has no book relation to be validated against; the induced-drag ratio is checked.
