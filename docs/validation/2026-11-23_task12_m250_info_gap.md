## Method

Ben-Haim's info-gap as in Marchau et al., DMDU, ch. 10. Uncertainty model (10.7), p. 213: theta_k in [theta_k0 - h W_k_lower, theta_k0 + h W_k_upper], weights W set so that h = 1 gives the ledger band ends (W_lower = nominal - band_lo, W_upper = band_hi - nominal; one-sided where the nominal is a band end). Robustness (10.8), p. 214: h_hat is the largest h at which the worst case over U(h) still meets the requirement, found by bisection on h in [0, 3]. Uncertain quantities: 53, the ledger assumptions that carry a band plus two engine multipliers (SFC and full-throttle power of the engine's table, +-10 % at h = 1). The SFC multiplier is applied on top of the ledger's SFC allowance (`engine_sfc_allowance` of the record's ledger copy), which stays in. The power multiplier is a **cap** on the available power only (owner ruling R-c, review M-2): the part-power fraction axis, and so SFC at a given shaft power, does not change.

**29 of 53 uncertain quantities act** on R5 or R7; 24 have exactly zero effect on every output at their band ends and are excluded from the headline count (listed below).

**Worst case search** (review IG-1, IG-3, DMDU p. 214-218 on global search for the inner maximum). The worst case over U(h) is not assumed to sit at a fixed corner. At every h the bisection tries, per requirement: (1) start at the one-at-a-time corner (each variable at its harmful end at h = 1); (2) coordinate search over ALL variables, each set in turn to whichever of {lower end, nominal, upper end} of its U(h) interval worsens the requirement most, sweeps repeated until no flip worsens, then pair flips of the ends of the most influential variables and the two engine multipliers (a mission that stops being flyable is a cliff that single flips cannot see; review IG-1), repeated at most 3 rounds; (3) an interior scan of 5 points per variable from that point, keeping any worsening. A mission that cannot be flown counts as 0 h, the worst possible. This is a search, not a proof; random sampling below checks it.

The aircraft is built: wing area, aspect ratio, tail scale and fuel load are the record's. Empty weight is uncertain (owner ruling, Eder 2026-11-22): the take-off weight is W0 = We(theta) + payload + the record's fuel, with We the component build-up at theta, iterated to a fixed point because some weight equations take W0. At nominal theta the fixed point gives W0 = 3001.134 lb against the record's 3001.132 lb (difference +0.0012 lb; within 0.1 lb: yes).

R5: hours on station with the record's fuel, the loiter time at which the rebuilt mission fuel (including the 6 % reserve factor) equals the record's fuel. R7: take-off and landing distance over 50 ft against the ledger field length. The engine power multiplier scales the take-off shaft power as well as the table's lapse.

## Exclusions (not in the horizon)

- Headwind strength (R19, 25 kt fixed) and Oswald efficiency e are outside the horizon (Eder, 2026-11-22)
- ISA temperature deviation is not varied (sea level and 20,000 ft are ISA)
- The engine control law is not varied (the table's throttle schedule is taken as is)
- The shape of the part-power SFC curve is not varied (only a uniform multiplier on every table entry)
- Trim drag (downwash and tail height) is not modelled

A 0 h point below is a mission that cannot be flown (the transit at 20,000 ft needs more power than the engine gives at that member of U(h)); the reason string is the model's.
