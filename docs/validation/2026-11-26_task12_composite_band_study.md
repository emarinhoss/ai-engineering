## The two bands (ledger `data/task12/ledger.yaml`; both mappings are judgements)

- **Composite band.** `composite_factor` 0.95, band 0.90 to 1.00 (Raymer p. 14, band per the owner's Q8). Raymer's Table 15.4 (p. 408) prints one factor per weight group (wing 0.85, tails 0.83, fuselage 0.90, landing gear 0.95) and no range. `composite_model: saving` scales the weight saving 1 - f of every group by (1 - c)/(1 - 0.95): 0.95 gives the printed factors, 1.00 a metal aircraft (as 1.00 does in the Table 3.1 fit), 0.90 twice the saving. `scale` (every factor times c/0.95, the probe of review GB-1) is run for comparison: at 1.00 it leaves the wing a 10.5 % saving and the gear none, so its band end is not a metal aircraft.
- **Empty-weight band.** `empty_weight_factor` 1.0, band 0.95 to 1.05, a multiplier on the summed build-up. The study already moved the empty weight through the inputs of the weight equations (gust speed, avionics, fineness, thickness, gear and sponson terms: review IG-2); it had no band on the equations themselves. The +-5 % is a judgement; the ledger line gives its basis (where the statistical fits and Raymer's Table 15.2 method sit against the build-up in the frozen record, and Raymer p. 409's 5 % growth figure).
- For h above 1 the composite factor stays at 1.00: the study clips a dimensionless fraction at 1 (`measurement/info_gap.uncertain_variables`). The empty-weight factor is not clipped.

## Limits

- Both mappings and the +-5 % empty-weight band are judgements, stated in the ledger; the library gives no range for Table 15.4 and no accuracy for the (15.46)-(15.57) equations. The comparison cases show how far the answer depends on them.
- The empty-weight multiplier acts on the summed weight only: the component list and the centre of gravity are not changed, and R13 was not re-checked at the band ends.
- Each case's `modes.json` flags a dirty tree: the only entry is the untracked output folder of this run. `summary.json` records that the tree was clean when the run started.
- The worst case is a search, not a proof (as in every info-gap record of this task); the random sampling counts are given above.
- No re-size was run. What a design sized to h_T 1.05 with these bands would weigh is not known from this record.
