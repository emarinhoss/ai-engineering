# Task 12: the re-sized cell and two alternatives under both bands (ruling B-2b)

Every number is read from `runs/task12/stage3/2026-11-26/m250_bands_cells/summary.json`, which is extracted from the record folders named in the table (`info_gap.json`, `cell_result.json` or `target_result.json`, `robust_candidate.json`, `design_pusher.json`). Generated by `python -m measurement.bands_cells --report-only`. No cell is recommended here; the owner chooses.

## What was run

- The re-size `runs/task12/stage3/2026-11-26/m250_bands_resize` (target h_T 1.05, composite band `saving` and empty-weight band acting, 450 shp M250-B17F as is) recorded the design of its lightest cell only. The other cells here are compliant candidates of its last iteration: each is sized again by the same function at the L_sized the re-size converged to (no new bisection) and the result is checked against the recorded row (W0 and fuel equal to 1e-9).
- Each then gets what the winner got: the endurance and transit-cliff mode scans, the mode searches at h_T and the full `measurement.info_gap.study` (seed and sample count in each `info_gap.json`). The winner's column is its own record, not re-run.
- The corner sets of the re-size were grown from the worst points of the designs it selected (W/S 17 A 7, then W/S 14 A 8). An alternative's L_sized was bisected against those sets, not against its own mode searches; whether its guarantee reaches the target is therefore a result below, not a construction.
