## What this requirement does not cover

- How the protection is built: sensors, air-data accuracy, control law, authority, failure cases (PD-11).
- Dynamics: the dive is a point mass on a straight path; no pushover, recovery pull-up, pitch response or turbulence spectrum.
- Lateral-directional limits (bank, sideslip, roll rate): R1 to R19 have none (PD-11).
- The engine's own limits beyond its table: operating altitude and relight stay declared assumptions (R-f).
